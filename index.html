TermDefinition

Home port / round-tripThis cruise departs from and returns to the same city — Boston — rather than ending somewhere different.
Overnight port callA stop where the ship stays docked into the next day, giving two separate excursion days at one port — Québec City on this cruise.
Sea dayA day with no port call — the ship is underway all day, and no shore excursions are offered.
Shore excursionAn optional, separately booked tour or activity at a port, run by a local operator and sold through the cruise line.
ShorexCruise-industry shorthand for "shore excursion" — shows up in the booking URLs throughout this guide.
